Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

Call Capital LLC ("Call Capital," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit callcapital.io or use any of our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name and email address when you subscribe to our newsletter or contact us
  • Payment information when you purchase a membership (processed securely by Stripe — we do not store card details)
  • Phone number and other details you voluntarily provide through our contact form
  • Communications you send us via email or contact forms

We also automatically collect certain information when you visit our site, including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring URLs through standard analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process your membership and deliver purchased services
  • Send the Call Capital newsletter and community updates (only if opted in)
  • Respond to inquiries and support requests
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Stripe — payment processing. Your payment data is handled directly by Stripe under their privacy policy.
  • Beehiiv — newsletter delivery. When you subscribe, your email is stored with Beehiiv.
  • Skool — community platform. Membership access is managed through Skool.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve your experience and analyze site traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain site functionality.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. If you cancel your membership, we may retain certain records for accounting or legal purposes.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at contact@callcapital.io. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. Contact us immediately if you believe a minor has submitted personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
