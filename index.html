FAQ

Common questions

What exactly is Call Capital?+

Call Capital is an operator-led membership ecosystem built on top of a real, operating call marketplace generating $2M+/month. It's an education platform, a community, and a participation platform where members can plug in as publishers, agents, brokers, or buyers. It is not a course.

I'm an insurance agent — is this for me?+

Yes. If you're buying CPL leads and want to understand how inbound calls actually work — who generates them, how they're routed, and how to get direct access to quality supply — this will change how you operate. Inner Circle members can plug in as agents and close inbound calls directly, earning commission on every policy.

I have no experience in the call space. Can I still join?+

Yes. The free content and Core membership are designed to take you from zero to operational. The playbook vault, biweekly live sessions, and community give you everything you need to understand the model and start building.

What's the difference between Core and Inner Circle?+

Core ($149/mo) gets you inside the business — the full playbook vault, community, and Deal Board. Inner Circle ($497/mo) adds biweekly 60-minute live sessions with recordings, private group access, co-build sessions, broker platform access, and agent seat access. Inner Circle is capped at 20 members.

What is the broker platform?+

Inner Circle members get access to our call distribution infrastructure — the same platform routing 50,000+ calls per month. Use it to build your own call brokering operation: source supply, route to buyers, and earn on the spread without building the technology from scratch.

Can I cancel anytime?+

Yes. No contracts. Cancel from your account anytime. We offer a 30-day refund on your first month if you don't find value.
