FAQ
Common questions
What exactly is Call Capital? +

Call Capital is an operator-led membership ecosystem built on top of a real, operating call marketplace generating $2M+/month. It's three things at once: an education platform, a community, and a participation platform where members can plug in as publishers, agents, brokers, or buyers. It is not a course.

What's the difference between Core and Inner Circle? +

Core ($149/mo) gets you inside the business — the full playbook vault, community, and the Deal Board. Inner Circle ($497/mo) adds biweekly 60-minute live sessions, recordings, private group access, co-build sessions, broker platform priority, and agent seat access. Inner Circle is capped at 20 members.

I'm an insurance agent — is this for me? +

Yes. If you're buying CPL leads and want to understand how inbound calls actually work — who generates them, how they're routed, and how to get direct access to quality supply — this will change how you operate. Inner Circle members can plug in as agents and close inbound calls directly.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. No contracts. Cancel from your account anytime. We offer a 30-day refund on your first month if you don't find value.

I have no experience in the call space. Can I still join? +

Yes. The free content and Core membership are specifically designed to take you from zero to operational. You don't need experience — you need the willingness to execute.
